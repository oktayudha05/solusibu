# Riset: pemutih + pembersih porselen jangan dicampur (slug `pemutih-porselen`)
Sumber awal: riset-malam/R3-topik.md §3.3. Dicek ulang 29 Okt 2026 (skill buat-short, uji): WA DOH, CDC MMWR, Poison Control dibuka, kutipan cocok. Yang dipakai di naskah cuma klaim ✅.

## Klaim yang dipakai
| # | klaim di naskah | tanda | sumber & kutipan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pemutih (klorin) dicampur produk asam → keluar gas klorin | ✅ | WA DOH: "When chlorine bleach is mixed with an acid, chlorine gas is given off." https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/contaminants/bleach-mixing-dangers · CDC MMWR: "When sodium hypochlorite and an acid are mixed, chlorine gas and water are released" https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/00015111.htm |
| 2 | Sebagian pembersih kloset/porselen isinya asam | ✅ (bingkai "ada yang") | Poison Control: "Many household cleaners contain acids, including vinegar and some toilet bowl cleaners." https://www.poison.org/articles/chlorine-gas · kasus MMWR: pembersih asam fosfat 4% + pemutih |
| 3 | Kadar rendah pun bikin perih mata/tenggorokan, batuk, sesak | ✅ | WA DOH: "even at low levels and short periods of time, almost always irritates the mucous membranes" · "causes coughing and breathing problems, burning and watery eyes" |
| 4 | Baca label produk sebelum dipakai | ✅ | WA DOH: "Be sure to always read the product label before using a cleaning product." |
| 5 | Terlanjur kehirup: keluar, cari udara segar | ✅ | Poison Control: "Leave the area immediately and breathe fresh air." |
| 6 | Ruangan ditinggal sampai udaranya berganti | ✅ | CDC MMWR: "areas in which the gas could circulate should be evacuated until sufficient air exchanges have occurred" |
| 7 | Ada gejala (sesak) → ke tenaga kesehatan | ✅ | WA DOH: "contact a health care provider or emergency response service" · Poison Control: get medical attention if trouble breathing |

## Sengaja TIDAK dipakai
- "Bisa langsung mati" (cuma kadar sangat tinggi, WA DOH) → pakai "sesak, bahaya".
- "Ga bau = ga aman" (✅ MMWR, tapi bikin alur lompat) · "satu produk satu waktu / bilas dulu" (⚠️ ga dikutip lembaga) · nama merek · "pemutih + deterjen = klorin" (❌).
- Klorin + air di saluran napas jadi asam (MMWR cuma bilang "the water", bukan saluran napas).

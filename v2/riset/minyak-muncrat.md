# Riset: kenapa minyak muncrat kena air (slug `minyak-muncrat`)
Sumber utama: riset-malam/R3-topik.md §3.1 (verifikasi putaran 2). Yang dipakai di naskah cuma klaim ✅. Dicek ulang 28 Okt 2026 malam: PDF FSIS dibuka & dibaca teksnya, halaman IU dibuka.

## Klaim yang dipakai
| # | klaim di naskah | tanda | sumber & kutipan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Minyak goreng panasnya sampai ±190°C (rentang 160-191°C) | ✅ | FSIS "Deep Fat Frying and Food Safety" (PDF): tabel suhu 320-375°F = 160-191°C. https://nps.edu/documents/111291366/111353839/Deep_Fat_Frying.pdf/1fa7c519-e7ff-48df-baa4-638206706f78 |
| 2 | Air mendidih di 100°C | ✅ | umum (tekanan 1 atm); Univ. Illinois Physics Van https://van.physics.illinois.edu/ask/listing/1734 |
| 3 | Air lebih berat dari minyak, jadi tenggelam ke dasar | ✅ | Damkar Kutai Kartanegara: "Air memiliki massa jenis yang lebih tinggi dari minyak" https://damkarmatan.kukarkab.go.id/index.php/panduan-aman-saat-wajan-terbakar-hindari-air-coba-cara-ini/ |
| 4 | Air ketemu minyak panas langsung jadi uap, uapnya bikin minyak muncrat | ✅ | FSIS PDF: "When water encounters very hot oil (about 350 °F) water vaporizes instantaneously turning into super heated steam. It expands quickly, which can cause oil to splatter" |
| 5 | Uap ngembang sekitar 1.600 kali (uap 100°C) | ✅ | Univ. Illinois Physics Van (link #2): "water expands by a factor of 1600" · SFPE NZ TP 2004/1: "approximately 1,600 times the original liquid volume" https://www.highrisefire.co.uk/docs/sfpe-TP2004-1.pdf |
| 6 | 1 sdm air (15 ml) jadi kira-kira 24 liter uap | ✅ (hitungan) | 15 ml × 1.600 = 24 L, dari #5. Diucapkan "kira-kira" karena angka itu buat 100°C, 1 atm |
| 7 | Keringkan bahan sebelum digoreng biar ga muncrat | ✅ | FSIS PDF: "pat dry in order to prevent oil splatter when food is submerged" |
| 8 | Masukin bahan pelan, jangan dijatuhin | ✅ | Indiana University Safety: "Add food gently into the pan to prevent splashing." https://safetystore.iu.edu/cooking-safety |
| 9 | Minyak kebakar: jangan disiram air | ✅ | USFA flyer: "Never try to stop a grease or oil fire with water." https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/fief/handouts/fief_cooking_safety_flyer_custom.pdf · FSIS PDF: "never use water to put out the fire" |
| 10 | Wajan jangan dipindah | ✅ | USFA pictograph: "Do not move the pan." https://www.usfa.fema.gov/gallery/pictographs/pictograph34.html · BPBD DIY https://bpbd.jogjaprov.go.id/berita/cegah-dan-ketahui-upaya-penanganan-kebakaran-minyak |
| 11 | Matikan kompor | ✅ | USFA pictograph (link #10): "Turn off the burner." · BPBD DIY |
| 12 | Tutup pakai tutup logam | ✅ | BPBD DIY: "Tutup api dengan penutup berbahan logam." · FSIS PDF: "covering the fryer with a metal lid" · USFA flyer: "Slide the pan lid or a baking sheet on top of the pan." |

## Sengaja TIDAK dipakai
- "1.700 kali" (angka damkar, dikoreksi di R3) → pakai 1.600.
- Hack garam anti-muncrat (❌ ga ada sumber ilmiah), "tutup 20 menit" (⚠️ cuma media), "tutup kaca pasti pecah" (⚠️).
- "Masukin menjauh dari badan": cuma ada di blog/penjual minyak (Frymax, Tasting Table), bukan lembaga → diganti "pelan, jangan dijatuhin" (IU).
- "Dasar wajan paling panas": belum ada sumber primer, ga diucapkan.
- Klaim kesehatan minyak/jelantah.

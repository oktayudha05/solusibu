# Riset: nasi sisa dipanasin lagi, aman? (slug `nasi-sisa`)
Sumber utama: riset-malam/R3-topik.md §3.2 (verifikasi putaran 2). Yang dipakai di naskah cuma klaim ✅. Dicek ulang 29 Okt 2026 dini hari: halaman FSA dan CDC MMWR dibuka, kutipan cocok.

## Klaim yang dipakai
| # | klaim di naskah | tanda | sumber & kutipan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Beras (mentah) bisa bawa spora bakteri *Bacillus cereus* | ✅ | FSA UK Home food fact checker: "Uncooked rice can contain spores of a bacterium called Bacillus cereus." https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/home-food-fact-checker/home-food-fact-checker · CDC MMWR 1993: "B. cereus is frequently present in uncooked rice" https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/00025744.htm |
| 2 | Dimasak pun sporanya tetap hidup | ✅ | FSA: "The spores of Bacillus cereus can survive being cooked." · CDC: "heat-resistant spores may survive cooking" |
| 3 | Nasi dibiarin di suhu ruang → bakterinya berkembang biak dan bikin racun | ✅ | CDC: "If cooked rice is subsequently held at room temperature, vegetative forms multiply, and heat-stable toxin is produced" |
| 4 | Racunnya ga hilang walau dipanasin lagi; tahan dipanaskan 121°C setengah jam | ✅ | FDA Bad Bug Book 2nd ed. (cereulide) "stable after heating at 121°C for 30 minutes" https://www.fda.gov/media/83271/download · CDC: toxin "can survive brief heating, such as stir frying" |
| 5 | Gejalanya mual, muntah | ✅ | FDA Bad Bug Book: tipe muntah, mual-muntah 0,5-6 jam; "vomiting-type outbreaks generally have been associated with rice products" |
| 6 | Jangan biarin nasi dingin di rice cooker | ✅ | FSA: "don't leave rice in the rice cooker, steamer or pan to cool down." |
| 7 | Sisa nasi dibagi ke wadah kecil, masuk kulkas secepatnya, idealnya ≤1 jam | ✅ | FSA: "chill it as quickly as possible, ideally within one hour" (+ saran porsi kecil di R3 §3.2) |
| 8 | Habisin dalam 24 jam (sehari) | ✅ | FSA: "Put the rice in the fridge and consume within 24 hours." |
| 9 | Panasin sekali aja, sampai ngebul (mengepul merata) | ✅ | FSA: "never reheat rice more than once"; panaskan sampai mengepul merata (R3 §3.2) |
| 10 | Masalahnya di cara nyimpen | ✅ | FSA: "It's not the reheating that causes the problem, but the way the rice has been stored before it's reheated." |

## Sengaja TIDAK dipakai
- "Nasi bertoksin ga beda bau/rasa/tampilan" (❌ sumber RBKC 404).
- "Tahan 121°C 80/90 menit" (NZ MPI ⚠️) → pakai FDA: 121°C 30 menit.
- Angka suhu mode warm rice cooker (⚠️), "nasi sisa pasti beracun", pati resisten/klaim kesehatan.
- Kasus MBG/BBPOM (⚠️ paywall).
